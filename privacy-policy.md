# Halma Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 15, 2026**

This policy explains how the Halma mobile game (the “App”) handles
information. The App is published by Ahmad Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

## Information handled by the App

The App does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name,
email address, or other contact details. We do not operate an App server, and
the App does not include our own analytics or tracking service.

To support resuming an interrupted game, the App stores the current match
settings and board state in the device’s local app preferences. This saved
match remains on the device until it is replaced by a new match, removed when
the match finishes, or the App’s local data is cleared. We do not receive this
saved game.

## Advertising

The Android version uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner ads and
occasional interstitial ads. The iOS version currently does not request or
display ads.

When ads are requested, Google and its advertising partners may collect or
receive information from the device and ad interactions, such as device or
advertising identifiers, IP address, app and device information, approximate
location inferred from the IP address, diagnostics, and ad views or clicks.
Google may use this information to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and
personalize advertising, subject to its settings, policies, and applicable
law. We do not receive the saved match data through the advertising SDK.

For details about Google's data practices and controls, see
[Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
You can manage advertising settings through your device and Google account
where available.

The App does not currently provide its own in-app advertising consent or
personalization controls. Ad behavior and available controls may depend on
your location, device settings, and Google's services.

## Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Match data is stored locally as described
above. Information handled by Google for advertising is subject to Google's
retention and sharing practices described in its privacy policy.

## Children's privacy

The App does not ask users to submit personal information. Advertising
services may nevertheless process device information as described above. If
you are a parent or guardian with a question about information handled by
Google's advertising services, please consult Google's privacy policy and
contact options.

## Your choices

You can stop ad-related processing by not using the Android version of the
App. You can clear the App's local data using your device's settings, which
also removes a saved match. Device and Google account settings may provide
additional advertising controls.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. The
updated policy will be published at this page with a revised effective date.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the
[Halma GitHub repository](https://github.com/azeinn/halma). Please do not
include personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
